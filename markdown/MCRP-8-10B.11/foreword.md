# Foreword

The Marine Corps Reference Publication (MCRP) 8-10B.11, *Antenna Handbook*, consolidates practical guidance for Marines who plan, install, and maintain radio communications in austere environments. The handbook emphasizes field-expedient solutions and highlights how to balance performance, survivability, and logistics when selecting or improvising antenna systems. Although technology evolves, the principles of electromagnetic propagation, grounding, and safety remain constant. This foreword introduces the intent of the handbook—getting Marines on the air quickly with reliable, mission-ready antennas while reinforcing good engineering discipline.

Key themes for readers:

- **Mission focus:** Every technique and example ties back to reliable tactical communications.
- **Field practicality:** Construction details use materials that can be scrounged or carried easily by small teams.
- **Safety first:** Grounding, lightning protection, and proper handling of radiating systems protect Marines and equipment.
